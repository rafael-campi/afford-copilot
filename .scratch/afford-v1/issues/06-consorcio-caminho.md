# 06: Consórcio Caminho

**What to build:** The comparison shows a Consórcio card with payment, total cost, Janela de contemplação (best case with Lance, median, worst case) and the chance of Contemplação by the Prazo.

**Blocked by:** 03 (Financing simulation from a structured Objetivo)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Taxa de administração and Fundo de reserva are spread over the Duração
- [ ] The Carta de crédito is adjusted yearly by a configurable index
- [ ] Contemplação by draw uses a configurable number of awards per assembly to compute best case, median, worst case and P(Contemplação ≤ Prazo)
- [ ] Lance livre and Lance embutido are modeled so follow-ups can simulate a Lance
- [ ] Fee parameters come from versioned market-range configuration and carry a Ressalva
- [ ] Simulador tests cover costs, adjustment and the contemplation probabilities
